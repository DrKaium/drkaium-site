Study in Russia

Guidance for international students, from a graduate of Saint Petersburg State Paediatric University who went through the process himself.

About this service

I came to Russia as an international student and graduated with an MBBS in 2026. I know how confusing the first steps can be, from choosing a university to preparing your documents and settling into a new country.

If you are thinking about studying in Russia, I can help you understand the process and avoid common mistakes.

How I can help

  • Choosing a university and courseHelp finding a university and programme that fits your goals and budget.
  • Admission application supportGuidance through the application process and preparing your application.
  • Document preparationChecking which documents you need and how to prepare them.
  • Certified translationHelp arranging certified translation of your documents for use in Russia.
  • Arrival and student lifeGuidance on the visa process, housing and what to expect when you arrive.

Fees depend on the service and are agreed with you before any work starts. I cannot guarantee admission, a visa or a scholarship, because those decisions are made by universities and the authorities.
