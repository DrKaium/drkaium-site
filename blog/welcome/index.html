Welcome to my blog

19 October 2026

I’m Dr. Monirujjaman Kaium, a physician with a growing interest in surgery, cardiology, and internal medicine.

This is more than a personal website; it’s a space to document my journey in medicine, exchange knowledge, and grow through continuous learning.
